# Circle other revenue and ARC evidence test

Started 21 November 2026 after the user authorized Stage 4. Existing Q3 outcomes, including $181.8m ARC revenue, are already known. This is a retrospective investigation of available evidence, not a blind forecasting exercise.

## Questions and cutoffs

Reconstruct seven quarters of other revenue by the original disclosed categories. Evaluate Q2 2025 through Q3 2026 at the day-before-earnings cutoffs already verified in Stage 2. Use only earlier disclosed financial inputs for a prediction. Separate operating Other revenue from non-operating Other income (expense), net.

For ARC, separately establish the agreement date, first public announcement, total consideration, cash receipts, token mint/distribution/escrow events, contractual performance obligations, and recognition date. An event's economic date does not establish when an outside analyst could discover or authenticate it.

## Fixed initial forecasting comparisons

Use a flat previous-quarter forecast by category as the first baseline. Compare quarter-length-scaled prior category amounts and, where two eligible prior quarters exist, the previous category's percentage growth repeated once. Report missing eligibility rather than retrieve later comparative information as an earlier forecast input. Comparisons on different availability samples must have a common-sample view. Do not tune growth caps or weights to these outcomes.

Keep ARC out of a recurring quarterly run rate. Use original disclosures to bound available presale consideration. Exact projected recognition needs a contemporaneously observable, authenticated milestone plus a disclosed accounting allocation/progress rule. If that rule is unavailable, report scenarios or a range, not a point forecast presented as on-chain-derived fact. Illustrative recognition fractions 0%, 25%, 50%, 75% and 100% may show sensitivities, but 75% is not independently validated merely because the known outcome is near it. With known consideration, a single-case broad bound of zero to the unrecognized consideration is not a confidence interval.

## On-chain evidence requirements

Authenticate the chain and ARC token contract against official Circle/Arc sources before interpreting its transfers. Distinguish USDC gas, unrelated tokens named ARC, testnet activity, the native coordination token, genesis allocations and actual ERC-20 transfer events. Establish block timestamp, transaction hash, log index, emitting address and mint/transfer amounts. Do not count a transfer as a new sale or as accounting revenue without agreement/milestone linkage. Investor identity is unverified unless supported by an attributable source. Record public endpoint failures rather than claim the chain has no activity.

For historical usefulness, distinguish a contract/address known before earnings from an address found only after the result. Current explorer responses or node configurations cannot alone prove historical first-public availability. If no authenticated public token address is recovered, explicitly stop the revenue inference at that identification boundary and report what could and could not be tested.

## Other product drivers and retained revenue

Check official fee schedules and free access for CCTP transaction services and USYC-related fees. Usage, assets and token flows are candidate drivers, not automatic recognized revenue. Distinguish private integration acceptance/fee terms and variable performance-fee entitlement. Extract separately reported partner cost amounts where feasible and retain a lagged ratio bridge when platform economics remain unobservable. Product forecasts, original financial actuals and terminal checks must remain separate.

## Deliverables

Original public-source snapshots and retrieval/checksum manifest; product-revenue ledger with publication dates; ARC event timeline with evidence levels; public chain-access and token-identification results; baseline forecast errors and event scenarios; and a findings note explaining which parts of other revenue are predictable, trackable or private. Save all work locally in Circle. No trades, account changes or external publishing are requested.
