# Transaction revenue backtest and subscription factor check

Research date 21 November 2026. Outcomes through Q3 2026 are already known. This is an extension of Stage 4, not a blind test.

Use original dated transaction-category observations in Stage 4. Evaluate Q3 2025 through Q3 2026: five comparable quarterly targets with prior-quarter inputs. Q2 2025 is a calibration quarter because the IPO's Q1 transaction-services definition differs from later transaction revenue. Keep the $12.220m Q4 2025 spike in all headline scores. It is derived from annual less original nine months, not removed as an outlier.

Fixed comparisons before calculations: prior quarter flat; day-scaled prior quarter; repeated previous quarterly growth where two comparable prior quarters were public; observed current-quarter CCTP fees plus previous-quarter non-CCTP residual; observed current CCTP plus day-scaled lagged residual; early-quarter extrapolation using only the first 30 calendar days of current CCTP history plus flat residual. All coefficients are fixed, not fitted. CCTP's mapping into Circle GAAP transaction revenue is a conditional accounting bridge, not an independently disclosed component reconciliation. Do not put USYC performance fees in transaction revenue.

The late case uses only data dated within the completed target quarter and financial disclosures public by the day before its earnings release. The early case uses first-30-day on-chain observations and only financials already released by that cutoff. Where prior-quarter financials were not yet public, report the result unavailable rather than use later information. Also report an early case using the most recent genuinely public quarter as its base, preserving staleness and quarter-length adjustments.

Current DefiLlama histories are reconstructed present-day data. Historical timestamp filters do not prove the data/API/adapter was available at the time. An observation-based pre-earnings estimate is a nowcast, not a quarter-ahead forecast. Sources, chain coverage, missing dates and adapter implementation dates must be retained. Do not silently fill missing CCTP dates with zero or forecast redemption fees by applying one rate to all burns.

Subscription candidates: total stablecoin supply in USD, BTC price and USDC share of that supply. Compute same-date daily and quarterly-average correlations and correlations of quarterly changes. Use growth/rate changes rather than promote correlations between trending levels. USDC share mechanically contains the stablecoin-total denominator; test correlation rather than assume independence. Seven quarterly financial observations are insufficient to validate a three-factor regression, lag selection or optimized weights. Factor findings are exploratory; do not fit a multivariate subscription model.

Report signed errors, absolute percentage errors, MAE, MAPE and common-sample comparisons. Inspect measurable fees as a fraction of actual transaction revenue separately from forecast accuracy. Reconcile to original filing rows and independently recompute outputs and source hashes. Deliver a concise findings note and formula-linked workbook without modifying original Stage 2/3 results or the dated Q4 forecast.

Coverage outcome: the first lagged CCTP quarter, Q2 2025, contains 19 missing dates. Therefore the Q3 2025 hybrid is unavailable, and the headline common sample is the four quarters Q4 2025 through Q3 2026. Early common comparison has three quarters, Q1-Q3 2026. These exclusions follow the missing-data rule rather than forecast performance.
