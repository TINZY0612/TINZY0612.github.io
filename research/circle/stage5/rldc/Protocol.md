# RLDC projection experiment

Recorded 21 November 2026 before calculating this comparison. Seven historical quarters and six target quarters are available. Their outcomes have already been viewed in prior research, so this is a reconstructed, publication-filtered experiment, not a blind holdout.

Target: Circle's total distribution, transaction and other costs; RLDC = reserve income + operating other revenue - those costs. Preserve all original Stage 3 outputs and earlier dated forward forecasts.

Compare predefined low-parameter rules: (1) last-quarter costs in dollars, (2) last cost/reserve-income ratio, (3) up-to-three-quarter pooled cost/reserve ratio, (4) exponentially weighted cost/reserve ratios with fixed 0.5 decay, (5) a contract-informed platform-share change rule with lagged unexplained residual, (6) a Coinbase pre-Circle-report gross-USDC-income proxy plus lagged non-Coinbase costs, (7) the proxy with a lagged multiplicative reconciliation, and (8) the proxy with a lagged additive reconciliation. The platform rule is conditional on actually available comparable platform balances and uses a lagged Circle platform share, never a target-quarter Circle disclosure. If unavailable, label missing and compare on a common sample; do not fill using later comparative KPIs.

Source review before running the comparison: Circle's original annual Coinbase cost amount is rounded to $1.4bn and later quarter comparatives change. Therefore the headline partner models will calibrate an **unclassified residual** directly as total costs minus Coinbase's gross USDC-income proxy, rather than claim that this residual equals exact non-Coinbase costs. Rule 6 scales that residual by reserve income, rule 7 scales total costs by the change in partner gross USDC income, and rule 8 carries the dollar residual flat. The approximate separately disclosed Circle-to-Coinbase cost ledger remains a diagnostic. This preserves six comparable target quarters without inserting a spurious exact Q4 partner-cost amount. The platform rule adjusts the lagged cost ratio by half the change in Coinbase's available average product share, keeping unobserved Circle mix and contractual adjustments flat. It is a simplified sensitivity, not an exact contract reproduction.

For each historical cutoff use only filings/partner disclosures public before Circle's release. Use the latest previously public vintage of each calibration quantity, preserving later changed comparatives as separate ledger observations. Original target-quarter Coinbase disclosures may be available before Circle and may enter the late nowcast; they must not enter the earlier-information baseline. Distinguish publication timing and accounting definitions, especially corporate-balance USDC income versus stablecoin revenue.

Use the established Stage 3 publication-filtered SOFR reserve estimate for full-quarter nowcasts. Score cost MAPE, MAE, worst error and signed errors independently of revenue. Also report cost-only RLDC error using actual revenue explicitly as a diagnostic, and end-to-end RLDC using only eligible revenue assumptions. Flat historical other revenue must strip any previously recognized ARC amount rather than carry it indefinitely. Treat historical ARC annual-guidance-to-quarter timing as separate scenarios; no target-quarter recognized ARC amount enters a forecast.

Show six-quarter end-to-end errors with the Q3 2026 ARC surprise included, an underlying non-ARC result with clearly labeled scoring normalization, and the previously documented approximately $180m Q3 event scenario separately.
