How to read the error measures

MAPE · Mean absolute percentage error
The average size of the forecast’s percentage miss, ignoring whether it was too high or too low.
MAE · Mean absolute error
The average dollar difference between the estimate and reported reserve income, shown in USD millions.
Worst error · Largest absolute percentage error
The biggest percentage miss in any single tested quarter. This is an observed result, not a limit on future errors.

Lower is better for all three measures.

02 / FOLLOW THE CALCULATION

See exactly how the estimate is built

Where do the fund yields come from?

The Circle Reserve Fund’s original monthly SEC N-MFP3 reports contain dated business-day sevenDayNetYieldDate and sevenDayNetYieldValue observations. They are annualized net seven-day yields, not daily returns and not USYC yields.

Only filings public by the cutoff are used. Each calendar day takes the latest eligible observation on or before that day. Daily USDC balances supply the weights; weekends keep their balances and carry the last eligible yield.
